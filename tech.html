about me

i am a programmer with an expertise in immersive technology, currently living in mumbai, india.

i can speak and write english, hindi and pahadi. i have two cats. i love all art forms i.e. films, music, books and games.

projects i am the most proud of

  • betterunity: a fully-featured extension/toolkit for the unity editor which almost makes it the perfect game engine for me.
  • theeverythingbagel: a website that has several functions but mostly unrelated. allows you to fetch art works from the met or look up the latest usdjpy exhcange rates or fetch any bible verse.
  • 2d-3dfloorplangenerator: takes 2d cad floorplans and converts them into a 3d model within a few minutes.
  • unlearn: a 2d platformer that incorporates meta-fictional elements into the mix alongside a fun gimmick.
